# Visual Shot List — Part One (Scenes 01–10)

## Global visual style
Grounded contemporary cinematic realism; natural skin texture; restrained color grading; practical lighting; believable human motion; natural lens perspective; no glossy advertising look. Use the same seed/reference-image workflow if supported by the selected model. Generate shots as separate short clips and edit them together; do not ask a single generation to produce an entire scene with multiple complex actions.

## Character reference locks
- **Adam:** 34, short dark hair, neatly trimmed beard, brown eyes, average build; understated neutral workwear. Keep beard length, hairline, face shape and clothing consistent.
- **Mariam:** 30, dark eyes, elegant modest hijab outdoors, modest contemporary clothing; comfortable modest home clothes indoors. Keep face shape and hijab style consistent.
- **Daniel:** 35, Black American man, short hair, casual-professional wardrobe; friendly, expressive face.
- **Sophie:** 32, British woman, shoulder-length dark hair, polished professional wardrobe; composed, attentive expression.
Create approved still reference images first if a genuinely free image workflow is available. Do not invent a new face in every scene.

## Shot plan

### Scene 01 — A Better Morning
1. Wide establishing shot of a lived-in apartment kitchen in soft morning light; Adam reviews an engineering model on a tablet while Mariam prepares breakfast.
2. Medium two-shot across the kitchen counter, affectionate banter and eye contact.
3. Over-shoulder close-up of Mariam pointing at the model; insert shot of the tablet showing a generic structural diagram, no legible fake technical claims.
4. Close-up on Adam considering her suggestion, then a subtle smile.
**Continuity:** both at home; natural, equal partnership; no exaggerated romantic posing.

### Scene 02 — Before the Door
1. Medium shot at the apartment entryway as Mariam finishes preparing to leave.
2. Close-up of her arranging her hijab naturally in a mirror; keep it consistent with her canonical reference.
3. Adam hands her the folder; two-shot with playful expressions.
4. Wide shot as they leave together.
**Continuity:** hijab is worn outdoors; do not frame the act as a dramatic conversion or lecture.

### Scene 03 — The Small Detour
1. Street-level wide shot of an ordinary busy city sidewalk and transit stop, realistic pedestrians and traffic.
2. Adam notices an older man struggling with grocery bags and approaches respectfully.
3. Medium shot as Adam helps carry one bag to a nearby bench; keep body movement simple and plausible.
4. Mariam and a young woman compare the route map and platform sign.
5. Closing wide shot of the couple continuing their commute.
**Continuity:** help is ordinary and unshowy; avoid staged crowds staring or applauding.

### Scene 04 — The Model
1. Wide shot of a modern engineering office with a small team around a screen.
2. Close-up of Adam reviewing a structural model and spotting an issue.
3. Over-shoulder shot of a generic stress-analysis visualization.
4. Medium group shot as Daniel asks a question and Sophie asks for a comparison.
5. Adam starts a second case; the team collaborates.
**Continuity:** realistic office environment; diagrams should be generic rather than text-heavy.

### Scene 05 — The Kitchen Question
1. Office kitchen during a casual team celebration; a few colleagues in the soft background.
2. Daniel offers a drink; Adam politely chooses sparkling water.
3. Medium two-shot for the respectful conversation; expressions curious and relaxed.
4. Insert shot of two ordinary cups raised in a friendly toast, one clearly non-alcoholic.
**Continuity:** no shaming, no conflict, no exaggerated alcohol imagery. Keep the scene warm and conversational.

### Scene 06 — Room to Breathe
1. Wide shot of Mariam's architecture studio, design boards and courtyard model on the table.
2. Medium shot of Mariam presenting; colleagues listen.
3. Close-up of a physical courtyard model showing shade, accessible routes and seating.
4. Over-shoulder shot of lifecycle-cost charts with abstract, non-readable labels.
5. Reaction shot as a colleague reconsiders and engages with the evidence.
**Continuity:** Mariam is visibly the expert leading the design discussion.

### Scene 07 — A Real Question
1. Medium-wide shot in a quiet office corridor after a meeting.
2. Two-shot of Daniel asking a sincere question; Adam listens without defensiveness.
3. Close-up on Adam delivering the core line about boundaries not being rejection.
4. Two-shot ending in an easy, genuine laugh.
**Continuity:** no confrontation, no hostile body language; preserve both actors' distinct appearances.

### Scene 08 — Home Is Home
1. Wide shot of the same apartment kitchen in evening light, meal preparation underway.
2. Medium two-shot as Adam and Mariam chop vegetables side by side.
3. Close-up of their hands working safely with kitchen tools and the food on the counter.
4. Reaction shots for the family-size joke, then a relaxed shared laugh.
**Continuity:** Mariam is in comfortable modest home clothing and may be without her outdoor hijab; the scene is loving and dignified.

### Scene 09 — The Correction
1. Wide shot of a meeting room with the draft report on a screen.
2. Close-up on Adam comparing two versions of a generic engineering result.
3. Medium shot as he explains that the conclusion must be verified before release.
4. Sophie pauses distribution and opens a review checklist.
5. Two-shot conveying mutual respect and professional responsibility.
**Continuity:** no melodramatic accusation; the issue is handled transparently.

### Scene 10 — An Invitation
1. Wide shot of the office lobby near the end of the workday.
2. Daniel catches up with Adam and invites him and Mariam to dinner.
3. Medium two-shot as Daniel mentions non-alcoholic options; Adam responds appreciatively.
4. Close-up on Adam's warm, touched smile.
5. Closing shot of Adam leaving the lobby, hopeful about the friendship.
**Continuity:** the invitation is inclusive without making Daniel seem patronizing.

## Reusable negative guidance
Avoid face morphing, age changes, inconsistent facial hair, changing hijab style between outdoor shots, extra fingers, warped hands, unreadable pseudo-text, floating props, duplicated people, exaggerated facial expressions, artificial waxy skin, stereotypes, preachy staging, and abrupt costume changes.

## Editing and audio
- Assemble short clips in shot order with restrained cuts and natural room tone.
- Record dialogue separately per character using one tested, authorized voice configuration per character.
- Keep dialogue, ambience and music on separate tracks when possible.
- Do not lip-sync generated footage until voice timing and shot duration are checked.
- This shot list is a production plan, not evidence that footage has been generated.
