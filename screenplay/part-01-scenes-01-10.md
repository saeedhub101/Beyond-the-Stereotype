# BEYOND THE STEREOTYPE
## Part One — Scenes 01–10
**Target:** 12–15 minutes. **Dialogue:** English.

### Scene 01 — A Better Morning
**Location:** Adam and Mariam's apartment, morning.
Adam checks a structural model while Mariam prepares breakfast. She notices a questionable assumption and suggests testing a different load path. Adam tries it and sees she may be right.
**ADAM:** I was hoping breakfast would be the most complicated thing I had to solve today.
**MARIAM:** You married an architect. You knew there would be revisions.
**ADAM:** I thought revisions meant choosing curtains.
**MARIAM:** And now you have a better model. You're welcome.
They laugh; Adam saves the test instead of dismissing her idea.

### Scene 02 — Before the Door
**Location:** Apartment entryway.
Mariam finishes getting ready to leave and puts on her hijab. Adam hands her a folder she nearly forgot.
**ADAM:** Folder, keys, phone.
**MARIAM:** You forgot the most important thing.
**ADAM:** Your coffee?
**MARIAM:** My patience. I left it in the kitchen.
They share an affectionate smile and leave together.

### Scene 03 — The Small Detour
**Location:** City sidewalk and transit stop.
They notice an older man struggling with grocery bags and a young woman confused by a transit map. Adam asks before helping the man; Mariam checks the route with the young woman rather than assuming.
**OLDER MAN:** I thought I could manage all of these in one trip.
**ADAM:** Ambition is admirable. The bags seem to disagree.
**YOUNG WOMAN:** Thank you. I didn't want to get on the wrong train.
**MARIAM:** Let's check the sign together. It's easier when two people look.
They continue without waiting for praise.

### Scene 04 — The Model
**Location:** Engineering office.
Adam finds an assumption that makes a public infrastructure stress model look safer than it is. He explains the problem without blaming anyone and proposes a test.
**ADAM:** The numbers are consistent. The assumption behind them isn't.
**DANIEL:** So the model is confidently wrong?
**ADAM:** Confidently incomplete. That's easier to fix.
**SOPHIE:** Show us the alternative case. If it changes the result, we document it.

### Scene 05 — The Kitchen Question
**Location:** Office kitchen.
Daniel offers Adam an alcoholic drink at a team celebration. Adam politely chooses sparkling water. Daniel asks with genuine curiosity.
**DANIEL:** Is it a personal preference, or is it part of your faith?
**ADAM:** For me, my faith asks me to avoid intoxicants. I like being clear-headed, especially when people rely on my decisions.
**DANIEL:** Fair enough. I wasn't sure if asking would be rude.
**ADAM:** Asking respectfully is fine. You don't have to share my choice to understand it.
**DANIEL:** To clear heads and fewer bad spreadsheets.
**ADAM:** That I can absolutely support.

### Scene 06 — Room to Breathe
**Location:** Mariam's architecture studio.
Mariam presents a courtyard design with shade, accessible paths, and seating for people of different ages and abilities. A colleague worries about upfront cost; Mariam shows lifecycle data.
**COLLEAGUE:** The simpler version is cheaper on day one.
**MARIAM:** Yes. But people use a place over years, not just on opening day. Shade helps in hot weather, accessible routes help everyone, and durable materials reduce replacement costs.
**COLLEAGUE:** You have the long-term numbers?
**MARIAM:** I brought two alternatives. We can compare them honestly.

### Scene 07 — A Real Question
**Location:** Office corridor.
Daniel asks whether Adam's religious boundaries mean he does not want to be friends.
**DANIEL:** Sometimes I can't tell if you keep certain boundaries because of your faith, or because you don't really want to be around us.
**ADAM:** Those aren't the same thing. My faith shapes some choices I make. It doesn't stop me from caring about people or enjoying their company. A boundary about one thing isn't a rejection of you.
**DANIEL:** I'm glad I asked.
**ADAM:** Me too. If I ever seem distant, ask me before writing the whole story in your head.
They laugh easily.

### Scene 08 — Home Is Home
**Location:** Adam and Mariam's kitchen, evening.
Mariam is in comfortable home clothes. She and Adam cook together, teasing each other about their different approaches to chopping vegetables.
**MARIAM:** Those pieces are not the same size.
**ADAM:** They're a family. Families don't have to look identical.
**MARIAM:** That's your engineering defense?
**ADAM:** It's my dinner defense.
They laugh and work side by side.

### Scene 09 — The Correction
**Location:** Engineering meeting room.
Adam discovers a flawed assumption in a draft report and asks Sophie to pause distribution until it is verified.
**ADAM:** It changes the margin in one scenario. I don't know yet whether the conclusion changes, so we shouldn't imply that it doesn't.
**SOPHIE:** Then we verify it, document the reason, and update the report. Thank you for catching it now.
**ADAM:** I'd rather have an uncomfortable meeting than leave someone relying on a number I don't trust.

### Scene 10 — An Invitation
**Location:** Office lobby, end of day.
Daniel invites Adam and Mariam to dinner with friends and mentions non-alcoholic drinks.
**DANIEL:** A few of us are having dinner on Saturday. Nothing formal. You and Mariam should come.
**ADAM:** We'd like that. Let me check with her.
**DANIEL:** I'll make sure there's plenty to drink that isn't alcohol.
**ADAM:** That's thoughtful. You don't have to redesign the whole evening around us.
**DANIEL:** I'm not redesigning it. I'm making room for my friends.
Adam smiles, touched.

## End of Part One
Continue with the dinner and friendships deepening through shared work and everyday life.
